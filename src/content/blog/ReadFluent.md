ReadFluent is a bilingual e-book reader for iPhone, iPhone Duo, and iPad.

**Read the original. Understand the translation.**

Import an EPUB you already own and read it in the language it was written in. When you want the meaning of a page, ReadFluent translates that page with the system translation on your device. On a wide landscape window, such as an iPad or an open iPhone Duo, the original and the translation sit on facing pages. On a phone, you switch between the original page and the translated page.

You can pick the language you are reading toward. A bookmark saves the passage, not a screen position, and opens that place again later.

The page can be warm white, paper sepia, or charcoal. That choice stays on the device.

ReadFluent is the app I wanted while reading a book in a language I was still learning: the original stays in front of me, and the translation is there when I need it.
