Hi, I'm Jorge Villanueva, a Senior Mobile Engineer based in Chicago. I've spent more than 10 years taking mobile apps from idea to the App Store, mostly on iOS, and more recently across iOS, Android, and React Native.

This page is a quick way to find what I'm working on and what I've built.

### What I do

- **Native iOS:** Swift, SwiftUI, Combine, UIKit, and, going back far enough, plenty of Objective-C.
- **Native Android:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Coroutines and Flow, and Android Jetpack libraries such as ViewModel and Navigation.
- **Cross-platform:** React Native, including turbo modules and native modules on iOS and Android.
- **Shipping:** CI/CD, bi-weekly releases, A/B testing, profiling, and keeping a codebase healthy over many years.
- **People:** mentoring engineers, especially web and React Native developers who are new to native mobile.

### Professional apps

**HomeAware** · Ubiety Technologies · [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/homeaware/id6480478470) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.residencewatch.mobile)  
A home awareness app that tells you who is in your home, whether residents, frequent visitors, or strangers, without indoor cameras or facial recognition. It only notifies you when something matters.

**eSphere** · Ubiety Technologies · [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/esphere/id6762152449) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ubiety.esphere)  
A presence-aware home app, offered through internet service providers, built on the same platform as HomeAware. It pairs with plug-in Halo Connect sensors and uses ambient Wi-Fi and Bluetooth signals to show when family, guests, and untrusted visitors come and go, with no microphones or cameras.

**Pangea** · Pangea Money Transfer · [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pangea-smart-money-transfers/id905853115) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pangea.android)  
A money transfer app for sending money abroad to 24 countries, with low fees, real-time tracking, and tools to organize transfers and track family savings goals. Rated 4.8 from more than 40,000 ratings on the App Store. I was the founding iOS engineer and led the app for ten years.

**Cebuana Lhuillier Quikz** · Pangea Money Transfer · [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cebuana-lhuillier-quikz/id1448407543) · [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pangea.cebuana)  
A partner app, powered by Pangea, for sending money to the Philippines, for cash pickup at more than 2,500 Cebuana Lhuillier branches or deposit into a Filipino bank account.

### Personal projects

**[ReadFluent](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/readfluent/id6822424096)** · iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad  
A bilingual reader for books you already own. Read in the original language and tap Translate to see the same page in another language. On a wide screen the original and the translation sit side by side like an open book. It collects no data. ([More about it](/blog/readfluent))

**[Did you know pkmn?](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/did-you-know-for-pokemon-lite/id608034349)** · iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch  
A trivia app with 750 facts, myths, and tips about the Pokémon world, with a daily fact, a Notification Center widget, and an Apple Watch app. It works fully offline.

**[SakuraPetalsView](https://github.com/kinejara/sakuraPetalsiOS)** · Swift library  
A lightweight, dependency-free library that renders falling cherry blossom petals with `CAEmitterLayer`. It works in both UIKit and SwiftUI, lets you configure speed, scale, and spin, and installs with CocoaPods.
