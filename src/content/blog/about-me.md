Hi, I'm Jorge Villanueva, a Senior Mobile Engineer based in Chicago. I've spent more than 10 years taking mobile apps from idea to the App Store, mostly on iOS, and more recently across iOS, Android, and React Native.

This page is a quick way to find what I'm working on and what I've built.

### What I do

- **Native iOS:** Swift, SwiftUI, Combine, UIKit, and, going back far enough, plenty of Objective-C.
- **Cross-platform:** React Native, including turbo modules and native bridges on iOS and Android with Kotlin.
- **Shipping:** CI/CD, bi-weekly releases, A/B testing, profiling, and keeping a codebase healthy over many years.
- **People:** mentoring engineers, especially web and React Native developers who are new to native mobile.
